# 15.4 Phase-1 Day3: regression and background audit

Day3 adds read-only audit contracts. It does not run or select a formal
intensity variant.

The three threshold-dependent point universes use `(frame_id,
raw_lidar_point_index)` identity:

- all post-intensity points;
- points inside positive Car GT;
- annotation-excluded background points.

Annotation exclusion covers Car, Van, Truck, Pedestrian, Cyclist,
Person_sitting, Tram, and Misc boxes with valid 3D geometry. DontCare is not
claimed as guaranteed background and is not used as a 3D exclusion box.

Strict-background lineage continues the 15.3.1 definition: the raw cluster has
no positive-Car association and no point-level overlap with any exclusion
annotation. Each unique detection identity is followed through Car
classification, NMS, and FP status at IoU 0.50 and 0.25.

TP and candidate regression audits operate on `(frame_id, gt_id)` sets and
store counts, ordered identities, and SHA-256. Stable-path regressions are
classified into the five frozen reasons; no unexplained result is silently
accepted.
